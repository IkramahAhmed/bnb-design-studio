About us

BnB Design Studio

Architectural Design & Engineering Coordination. Your Dream, My Design.

BnB Design Studio is an Architectural Design Studio based in Wylie, Texas, providing comprehensive architectural design, structural design, and Mechanical, Electrical & Plumbing (MEP) design services throughout the Dallas-Fort Worth (DFW) Metroplex.

Since 2019, we have been working with property owners, developers, business owners, contractors, and investors to transform their ideas and project requirements into practical, functional, and well-coordinated design solutions.

Our primary focus is on providing complete architectural and engineering design documentation, including construction and city permit drawing sets for commercial and residential projects. We understand that every project has its own requirements, challenges, and objectives. Our approach is to develop designs that balance functionality, aesthetics, building code requirements, and construction practicality.

Our approach

Planning, communication, coordination.

At BnB Design Studio, we believe that successful projects begin with proper planning, clear communication, and well-coordinated drawings.

We work closely with our clients, contractors, consultants, and engineering professionals throughout the design process to help ensure that project documentation is complete, practical, and ready for construction and permit review.

From initial concepts and existing building surveys to detailed construction documents and permit submissions, we aim to provide our clients with dependable design support throughout their projects.

Our commitment

Concept to reality.

Our goal is simple: to provide thoughtful architectural design, coordinated engineering documentation, and reliable permit drawing services that help bring our clients' projects from concept to reality.

Whether it is a small commercial renovation, a restaurant finish-out, a retail development, or a new construction project, BnB Design Studio is committed to delivering quality design and professional service.
