    About us

    Ideas into well-coordinated design.

    BnB Design Studio is an architectural design studio based in Wylie, Texas, providing architectural, structural and Mechanical, Electrical & Plumbing design throughout the DFW Metroplex.

    Since 2019 we have worked with property owners, developers, business owners, contractors and investors, turning project requirements into practical, functional designs that balance aesthetics, building code and construction practicality.
